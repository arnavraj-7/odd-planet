06 — Rules

How not to break it

Contrast pairs that pass
#F4F5F8 on #05060A — 18.4:1
#8A90A6 on #05060A — 6.2:1
#FFFFFF on #2D45F0 — 6.5:1
#FFFFFF on #2627A6 — 10.9:1
#DEE2FF on #03045E — 11.8:1
Do
  • Let black carry 80% of the page; blue is punctuation.
  • Hairline borders (#1A1E2B) instead of drop shadows.
  • Blue text only at blue/200 or lighter.
  • Radii 3–5px. Flat, sharp, agency — not soft SaaS.
Don't
  • Blue-to-purple gradients or glow blooms.
  • #2627A6 as text on black — it fails at 2.1:1.
  • A third accent hue. Blue and neutrals only.
  • Archivo for paragraphs, Instrument Sans for display.
